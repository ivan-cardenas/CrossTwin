Source: {{ source }} — knmi.nl/klimaatscenarios. The Klimaateffectatlas viewer offers the current climate, 2050 high and 2100 high; "high" is taken as the dry variant (Hd), the heat and drought case. Delta-change on the observed weather: a what-if, never stored. "Average day" adds the change of the average daily maximum (minimum at night), which understates heat waves: the hottest days warm more. "Warmest day of the year" uses KNMI's key figures per station: {{ stations_source }} — klimaatscenarios-data.knmi.nl. The portal does not define the warmest day; its 1991-2020 values match the warmest daily mean temperature. The rain event uses KNMI's extreme-rain figure.
